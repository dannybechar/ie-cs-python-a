# Audit: Unit 4 (conditional execution) against the ministry Classroom program

Audited 2026-11-13, read-only. Repo: `units/u4-conditional-execution/` (3 meetings, 270 min). Ministry: folder meetings 7, 8, 9 (+ 18 conditions part, see `audit-u5.md`). Hours authority: `docs/ministry-source/python-a.pdf` p.3-4, p.10.

**Verdict (5 lines)**
1. Unit 4 is sound on hours (6 h = 270 min matches PDF p.10) and on boolean expressions, `if/else`, compound `and/or`, one-level nesting, truth tables and tracing; the lessons are internally consistent.
2. BLOCKING: the repo says `elif` "is not in the official concept list", but python-a.pdf p.4 (continuation of the Chapter 4 row) lists "משפט ביצוע מותנה מתגלגל" and "הוראת תנאים באמצעות Turtle"; the ministry folder teaches it (9.1 slides 24-28, deck 9.4), the exam formula sheet contains `elif`, and exam-prep item 18.3 #1 uses it. No repo `.py` has `elif`.
3. SHOULD: `not` (ministry 8.1 slides 27-30, task "אי-זוגי"), digit extraction with `//` and `%` (needed by 7.2/7.3 tasks), max of two/three numbers, "find an input that gives Yes/No" tasks, the else-association rule, and ministry terminology ("פעולה", "תנאי מתגלגל", "אופרטור יחס").
4. Time: the ministry's own estimates are 2 lessons for meeting 7, 1-2 for 8, 2-3 for 9 (4-7 lessons) against 3 repo meetings; adoption must be selective and net-zero. About 29 min of additions are proposed, each paired with a named cut (table at the end); anything not funded goes to homework (0 class min).
5. Repo-internal: unit-strategy.md mis-cites the program overview (nested one-level is from PDF p.10, not p.3-5) and misses the rolling-conditional row; `course-map.md` "Open coverage gaps: None" is therefore wrong; 4 starters vs 3 references (Shapes_Starter pairs with Conditions_Reference).

Method note: the decks `m1-3-slides-he.pdf` are image-only (NotebookLM). I read the lesson notes and lab briefs in full and sampled the decks as contact sheets (4.1 slides 1-6, 4.3 slides 1-12); they match the notes. Remaining slides were not individually inspected.

Ministry decks 9.4 and the image-only slides were read from `extracts/ocr-m09-4.md` (present). Slides 3-5 of `materials-m07-4-528fca.md` (7.4 guided practice) have no text and are not in the OCR set: pending. 7.6 (flow-chart enrichment) is only named in `materials-m07-0-94cdc4.md` slide 2; no file for it exists in the import, so its content is not known.
