# Audit: Unit 7 (Algorithmic Problems: counter, accumulator, random)

Date: 2026-11-13. Read-only audit; no repo content was edited. "m16" always means the ministry **folder** meeting 16 (random, counter, accumulator, global, time loops), never repo meeting 16 (= 6.2). Meeting 19 and the formula sheet findings are in `audit-u6.md`.

**Verdict (5 lines)**
1. Topics and hours are correct: `python-a.pdf` p.15 gives counter 2 h, accumulator 1 h, random number 3 h (1 theory + 2 practice) = 6 h = 270 min, exactly what `course-map.md` plans; the patterns taught (start at 0, update inside `if`, accumulator, average, min/max, `randint`, simulation) match m16 `materials-m16-1-42299c.md` slides 4-21.
2. Two blocking deviations from the content authority: the repo forbids **`+=`** (ministry teaches it as "כתיבה מקוצרת" in slides 10 and 13 and uses it in most deck and worksheet code; the Q3 past-exam fragments use the long form) and forbids **`elif`** (ministry coin sorting, card game and flag-counting all use it) (U7-01, U7-02).
3. The ministry practises counters and accumulators mostly with **unknown-length `while` loops** (donations, storage space, card game) and **invalid-input branches**; Unit 7 uses only `range(n)` loops, and the reference `survey()` counts any answer that is not `y` as `n` (U7-03, U7-04).
4. The exam sheet lists `random.random()`, `randrange(start,end)`, `abs/max/min` and `+=`; Unit 7 excludes all of them (U7-05, U7-11). The ministry exam-style question 16.3 Q3 (marked "ממבחן מפמ"ר") is worth adopting in checkpoint B (U7-10).
5. All proposals fit in 270 min by swapping tasks (ledger in section 3); the Turtle random walk is repo-only but supported by the PDF ("הוראת בעיות אלגוריתמיות באמצעות Turtle", p.4) and kept; `global` and `return` stay in Unit 8, consistent with the PDF but not with m16 (U7-06, U7-07).
