- conditional repetition.
- `while`.
- stop condition.
- risk of non-termination.
- compare loop types.

## Meeting 3 — Lab + Lab
- loops with Turtle.
- patterns and repeated geometry.

## Meeting 4 — Lab + Lab
- tracing.
- identify appropriate loop type.
- determine iteration count.
- algorithmic loop challenge.
- integrated assessment.

## Exit criteria
The student identifies when a loop is required, chooses the appropriate
type, traces execution, and explains the stop condition.
